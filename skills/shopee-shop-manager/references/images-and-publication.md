# Images and publication

## Confirm identity, then create

Use owner pack photos/barcodes or confirmed exact manufacturer/distributor references. Log URLs/access dates. Photos can identify a brand without proving current stock, weight, expiry, local registration or reuse rights. Show a small labelled candidate board when needed. "All photos correct" is not an answer to a separate stock question.

Use source photos or generation according to owner policy and rights. If generated-only is required, use the available image-generation tool/skill, not raw supplier images. Follow its current instructions. If unavailable, state the limitation and complete independent preparation.

Preserve real brand, logo, mascot, flavour, form and product shape. Reject altered mascots or wrong packaging. Do not invent certifications, ingredients, origin, batch/expiry/regulatory numbers, health claims, shipping promises or prices. Do not remove a genuine warning to disguise category.

Make the sale unit clear: an enlarged sachet may illustrate a ten-sachet strip, but buyers must understand what they receive. Serving props are not included goods unless actually sold together. Match requested gallery count and current platform requirements; four images is an example, not a universal rule. Use the owner's style reference, not another shop's identity.

Keep one product folder with originals, finals, rejected versions and source/QA ledger. Use bounded previews and file links, not repeated originals or binary/base64 chat payloads.

## Listing facts

Verify identity/category/brand representation; approved price; available stock and allocation; inner quantity/net content/total; attributes and description; packed shipping measurements or explicitly accepted estimates; publication authority and any holds.

Inspect automatic prefills. They can insert wrong ingredients, shelf life, dietary badges or pack sizes. Clear unverified values. Do not select another brand to bypass a missing entry. Use a catalogue fallback only when accurate and permitted for this category/account; otherwise inspect the brand-request route and keep the question unresolved. Native success does not prove legal eligibility.

For food, supplements, medicines, cosmetics or other controlled categories, verify the exact product and jurisdiction using current primary evidence/supplier documentation. Not every herbal drink is medicine; overseas registration does not prove local eligibility. Preserve explicit holds and follow host rules for regulated-goods/manual actions. Never relabel a product as ordinary food to circumvent them.

## Draft and live states

Verify what Draft, Unpublished and Delisted mean here; do not promise the Draft tab when the actual workflow saves delisted. Before publication, verify images, final fields, unit-to-stock interpretation, shipping and that editing enabled no paid promotion/programme. Record native proof, reread final values, then check the public page where possible.

Keep reviewed IDs when correcting compatible products. A click or accepted form is not verified completion. On rejection, report the actual target-specific validation and resolve what is possible; do not invent a general platform ban or seek repeat permission for already authorised work.
