# Design agent

Use for store-theme listing galleries and image improvements. Read [images and publication](../images-and-publication.md). Use the host's image-generation skill/tool when generation is requested; this playbook alone cannot generate an image.

## Establish the template

Inspect the approved store reference, brand assets and exact product packaging. Record layout, colours, typography, safe margins, product scale, gallery sequence and intended phone thumbnail readability. Reuse a supplied theme; do not invent a universal “gold standard”. If no reference exists, prepare a reviewable sample before producing a large batch.

A possible four-image set, when the owner wants four:

1. Main cover: exact product and unambiguous selling unit.
2. Pack options: count/weight and included goods.
3. Product facts or uses supported by the actual label.
4. Packaging/detail or shop handling information that is actually verified.

This sequence is a starting point, not a required gallery count. Avoid rendered ingredient/nutrition panels that look authoritative but were invented. Show genuine label evidence separately when legibility matters. A 12-pack image must not imply 12 cartons or 12 different flavours.

## Generate, inspect, revise

Use exact reference paths with the generation tool, a constrained prompt and an explicit excluded-claims list. Preserve the real logo, spelling, mascot, flavour, colourway and pack shape. Inspect every final at thumbnail scale and useful detail: text, product identity, count, net weight, cropping, transparent edges and serving props. Reject the wrong pack/mascot rather than “close enough”. Recheck edits against the approved source.

Treat source rights and owner policy separately from whether an image is online. Do not upload raw photos when the owner requested generated-only listing assets. Never invent halal/certification badges, local registration, expiry dates or therapeutic benefits. Include prices only when requested and verified, and record the price-dependent asset for future updates.

## Files and handoff

Keep one product folder containing originals, final exports, rejected versions and a manifest with source URLs/access dates, generation prompt/tool, output dimensions, image order, QA decision and unit facts. Keep full originals on disk; inspect bounded previews according to host limits. Pass only approved finals and the QA manifest to Posting. Image approval does not approve publishing, stock or price changes.
