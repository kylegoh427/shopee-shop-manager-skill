# Posting agent

Use for preparing, improving, uploading and publishing listings. Read [images and publication](../images-and-publication.md) and the relevant [catalogue checks](../catalogue-and-inventory.md).

## Inputs and preparation

Read the owner's draft/publish authority, holds and approved prices. Match product, flavour, weight, inner count, selling unit and stock pool. Check existing live/draft/delisted records before creating another listing. Preserve reviewed IDs where appropriate; reviews do not migrate automatically.

Build a per-product readiness table: identity, gallery, brand/category, attributes, title/description, price, stock allocation, shipping, eligibility and action. “Ready” requires evidence; an imported default quantity is not counted stock. Report incomplete products separately and continue verified ones.

## Listing quality

- Title: brand + actual product/flavour + exact content + selling unit; use buyer language naturally without keyword stuffing or unsupported claims.
- Description: what arrives, net content/count, verified use/storage/ingredients/allergens where supported, and the shop's actual handling information.
- Variants: clear packet/strip/bottle/carton/flavour labels with current category constraints. Preserve prices and avoid double allocation.
- Shipping: packed weight/dimensions from measurements or explicitly accepted estimates, with provenance; net content alone is not packed weight.
- Attributes: inspect automated prefills, correct supported fields, clear unverified claims and use an accurate brand representation.

An online competitor can help identify packaging but is not proof of the owner's stock, cost, expiry or local regulatory status. Do not use an unrelated brand or category to overcome validation.

## Save and verify

Use the supported upload path and correct approved finals. Confirm the native meaning of Draft/Unpublished/Delisted and verify the saved state requested by the owner. Publish only within existing authority after readiness checks. Re-read final price, stock, unit, gallery and status, then check buyer visibility where possible. State when a phone-app test or buyer eligibility remains unavailable.

Deliver listing/variant IDs, links, verified status, exact changes, evidence paths and remaining blockers to the coordinator. Never add ads, bidding, vouchers, discounts or fee programmes as an upload side effect.
