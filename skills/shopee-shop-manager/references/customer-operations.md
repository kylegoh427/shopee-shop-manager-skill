# Orders, customer leads and reviews

## Order triage

Check time, account and last successful record. Compare stable order IDs, not just dashboard counts. Distinguish placed, unpaid, paid non-COD, approved COD, shipped, delivered, completed, cancelled and refunded. Cash-at-store awaiting payment is not approved COD. Delivered is not necessarily completed/Order Received. Escrow/income is not profit or necessarily today's newly placed sales.

For actionable orders report product, selling unit, units, merchandise amount, verified payment/approval and shipping deadline without buyer contacts. Flag deadlines; do not arrange shipment, refund or change status without authority. Exclude owner tests from business results.

## Customer enquiries and leads

Determine whether sending or only drafting is authorised. Match the shop, buyer/thread and product. Read the current message and any existing reply/bot answer first. Answer the buying question using verified stock, variant and pack details; offer the relevant listing or useful next step. Do not invent urgency, promotions, testimonials or delivery guarantees.

Match the customer's actual language, not presumed nationality or dashboard locale. Malay and Indonesian are related but distinct. Use the dominant language in a mixed message or ask a concise clarification when needed. Internal reports follow the owner's preference.

| Question | Useful response |
|---|---|
| Stock | Verify this variant and the quantity needed. |
| Shipping | State verified handling time; distinguish dispatch from courier arrival. |
| Expiry | Check current batch, not a reference photo's date; escalate if unknown. |
| Pack choice | Explain units and price per inner item without changing prices. |
| Wrong/damaged item | Acknowledge, request only needed evidence on Shopee, escalate within policy. |
| Health/allergens | Use exact label facts; do not invent suitability or medical advice. |

Keep payments/transactions on platform. Do not request personal information already available through Shopee. No unsolicited bulk messages or spam. If "spam" seems to mean free Bump, clarify the feature when it changes the action.

## FAQ and auto-replies

Review greetings, off-work replies, assistant/FAQ triggers and seller handoff together. Give each a distinct purpose; avoid stacked duplicate welcome/away messages. Use real staffed hours and an actual escalation process. Prepare accurate answers about stock, unit, dispatch, delivery estimates, expiry checks, tracking and damaged goods.

Test only with an owner-approved buyer account/thread when authorised. Check first contact, FAQ choice, handoff and off-work behaviour where possible. Do not message unrelated buyers or change shop hours simply to force a test. A settings screenshot is not buyer-side verification.

## Public reviews

Read exact review/rating/product and existing seller response. Skip already-replied reviews. Match written language; use the owner's default for star-only reviews. Tailor a short, truthful thank-you to the comment. Do not mention private order details, demand another rating, or give incentives.

Examples, not automatic claims:

- English: "Thank you for your review! We're glad your order arrived well packed. We appreciate your support."
- Malay: "Terima kasih atas ulasan anda! Kami gembira pesanan sampai dalam keadaan baik. Terima kasih kerana menyokong kedai kami."
- Indonesian: "Terima kasih atas ulasannya! Senang pesanan sudah diterima dengan baik. Terima kasih sudah berbelanja di toko kami."
- Simplified Chinese: "谢谢您的评价与支持！很高兴商品已妥善送达，期待再次为您服务。"

Say an order arrived well only when the comment/verified record supports it. For an empty review, thank the buyer for the rating/support without inventing a delivery experience.

Escalate low ratings, complaints, safety allegations, disputes and matters outside authority. A five-star review containing an unresolved complaint is not a routine cheerful thank-you. Do not promise refunds/replacements/compensation without authority. Verify native submission and displayed reply; save exact text, language, identity/time and compact evidence privately. Inspect uncertain success before retrying.

## Neutral review requests

Public review replies and buyer review requests are separate permissions. Only send a request if explicitly authorised and permitted by the platform. Verify the configured eligible completion event, no known dispute/return, no existing review, correct buyer/thread, and reliable evidence this request was not already sent. A dispatch or merely Delivered does not substitute for a required completed/Order Received event.

Deduplicate by shop, buyer, purpose and covered order IDs. One message can cover several newly eligible orders from the same buyer. If old chat/prior sends are uncertain, hold and report uncertainty. Exclude tests. Ask for an honest review without a rating target, voucher, coins or reward.
